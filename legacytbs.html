Texas Bridge Series Legacy Runners

Honoring Those Who Completed Every Race, Every Year

The Texas Bridge Series is one of the most unique running challenges in Texas—three races, three bridges, and one incredible finish. Starting with the Kemah Toughest 10K, continuing with the Galveston Toughest 10K, and finishing with the La Porte By the Bay Half Marathon, this series demands grit and determination.

Our legacy runners are those who have completed all three races in a single year, conquering the Kemah Bridge, Galveston Causeway, and the Fred Hartman Bridge. Their dedication keeps the tradition alive and inspires runners across Texas.

We proudly celebrate these champions of endurance who return year after year to complete the full Texas Bridge Series.
