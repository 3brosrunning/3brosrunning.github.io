Honoring Our Galveston Toughest 10K Runners

Celebrating Strength, Stamina, and the Legacy of the Causeway

The Galveston Toughest 10K is more than just a race across the Causeway—it’s a true test of grit, strength, and determination. Runners battle wind, incline, and distance to claim their victory atop the bridge.

Our legacy runners are those who return year after year to face this challenge, proving their dedication and resilience. Each finisher carries the spirit of endurance and helps grow the tradition of one of Texas’ most iconic 10Ks.

We are proud to honor those who have run this race five, ten, or even more times—especially those who stand as our top legacy champions. Your persistence inspires us all.

With gratitude,
The 3 Bros Running Company Team
